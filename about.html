About Khalid Mustafa Tech

Useful, privacy-respecting Android apps — made by one developer who cares about getting the small things right.

Who we are

Khalid Mustafa Tech is an independent Android app developer. We design and build small, focused apps that solve everyday problems — then polish them until they feel effortless. The idea is simple: small ideas, big impact.

What we build

Our Google Play catalog covers the tools people reach for every day:

  • Call recording — automatic, private call recording you can trust.
  • Video downloaders & savers — keep the videos and reels you have the right to, to watch offline.
  • Status savers — save the photo and video statuses you can already view.
  • Everyday utilities — QR scanner, flashlight, notes, money ledger, video tools and more.
  • Casual games — quick, relaxing puzzles and arcade fun.

What we believe

  • Privacy first. Wherever possible, your data stays on your device. We don't build apps around harvesting your information.
  • Free and accessible. Our apps are free to download, supported by ads so anyone can use them.
  • Light and fast. Small app sizes, clean design, no clutter and no sign-up.
  • Honest. We tell you plainly what an app does, what it needs, and why.
