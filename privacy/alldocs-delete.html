Data Deletion - AllDocs — PDF, Office & Image Converter

App: AllDocs — PDF, Office & Image Converter

Package: km.tech.alldocs

Developer: Khalid Mustafa Tech

Last updated: November 25, 2026

AllDocs stores the documents you convert, recent-file history and settings on your own device. There is no AllDocs account and no developer server that holds your data, so you are in full control and can delete it yourself at any time. This page explains how.

1. Delete items inside the app

  • Open AllDocs, go to your file list or history, and delete items you no longer want.
  • Converted files are saved to your device storage — open your Files app to delete those copies too.

2. Delete everything at once

  • Clear all app data: on your phone go to Settings > Apps > AllDocs > Storage > Clear data. This erases every item and setting the app holds on the device.
  • Uninstall: removing AllDocs deletes all data the app stored on the device.

Advertising data

This app shows ads through Google AdMob. Ad networks collect their own technical ad data (such as your advertising ID and IP address) directly; the developer never receives or stores it. To limit or delete this data, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of personalised ads in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads, and manage retention under Google's own privacy controls.

Request deletion by email

Because no data is held on a developer server, there is nothing stored for us to delete. If you still want to send a deletion request, or need help removing data from your device, contact:

Email: masterabdullahkhalid@gmail.com

Please include the app name (AllDocs — PDF, Office & Image Converter) in your message. We will reply with the steps above and help you complete the deletion. We aim to respond within 30 days.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: all data and settings the app stored on your device, once you follow the steps above.
  • Kept until you remove it yourself: any converted files saved to your device storage (delete them in your Files app)
  • Not held by us: the developer keeps no server copy of your data. Ad-network technical data is held by Google under its own policy and retention periods.

For full details, see the AllDocs — PDF, Office & Image Converter Privacy Policy.
