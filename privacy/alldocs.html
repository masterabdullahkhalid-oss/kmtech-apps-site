Privacy Policy - AllDocs — PDF, Office & Image Converter

App: AllDocs — PDF, Office & Image Converter

Package: km.tech.alldocs

Developer: Khalid Mustafa Tech

Effective date: October 29, 2026 (Last updated: October 29, 2026)

1. Introduction

AllDocs — PDF, Office & Image Converter ("AllDocs", "the App") is developed by Khalid Mustafa Tech. This Privacy Policy explains how the App collects, uses, and protects your information. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Files and Documents

The App processes files you select, including PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, images, and other document formats. All file processing is performed locally on your device. Your files are not uploaded to our servers.

2.2 Crash Reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

The App uses Firebase Crashlytics to collect anonymous crash and error reports. This data includes device model, OS version, and stack traces. Crash reporting is disabled by default and activates only after you provide consent. No personal data is included in crash reports.

2.3 Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

The App uses Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage statistics such as screen views and feature usage frequency. Analytics is disabled by default and activates only after you provide consent. No personal data is collected.

2.4 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads. You can opt out of personalized ads through your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization). The App does not access the Advertising ID (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID permission is explicitly removed).

2.5 Camera

The App requests camera access only when you use the document scanner feature. Camera data is processed locally and is not transmitted or stored beyond what you save.

2.6 Storage

The App requests storage permissions to read files you select and to save processed output. The App accesses only files you explicitly choose.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any personal identification.
  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not collect contacts, call logs, or messages.
  • We do not upload your files or documents to any server.
  • We do not track your browsing history.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Crashlytics — Firebase Privacy
  • Firebase Analytics — Firebase Privacy

5. Data Security

All document and image processing happens locally on your device. We do not operate servers that receive your files. We use industry-standard practices to ensure the App does not leak your data.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the App, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Offline Functionality

Most features of AllDocs work without an internet connection, including PDF tools, image editing, format conversion, and document viewing. Internet access is required only for: loading ads, downloading optional OCR language data, crash reporting (when enabled), and analytics (when enabled).

8. Data Retention

The App does not store your data on any server. Locally processed files remain on your device under your control. Crash reports and analytics data are retained by Firebase according to Google's data retention policies.

9. Your Rights

You can:

  • Deny camera and storage permissions at any time through device settings.
  • Opt out of personalized ads through your device's Google settings.
  • Disable crash reporting and analytics by declining consent in the App.
  • Delete all App data by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact Khalid Mustafa Tech at masterabdullahkhalid@gmail.com.
