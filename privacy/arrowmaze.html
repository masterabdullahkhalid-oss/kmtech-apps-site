Privacy Policy - Arrow Maze - Zig Arrow

App: Arrow Maze - Zig Arrow

Package: com.kmtech.arrowmaze

Developer: Khalid Mustafa Tech

Effective date: 18 November 2026

Arrow Maze - Zig Arrow is a minimalist puzzle game. Arrow Maze - Zig Arrow is developed by Khalid Mustafa Tech. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, or location.

  • No personal data you provide: The app does not ask you to enter any personal information.
  • Game data stored on your device: Your game progress, unlocked themes, settings, and scores are saved locally on your device only. This data is not transmitted to us.
  • Permissions: The app does not request any sensitive device permissions (no camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, storage, or phone access).
  • Automatically collected (by Google SDKs): anonymous app-usage statistics, device/diagnostics information, crash reports, and a mobile advertising/app-instance identifier, as described under Third-Party Services. This data is collected in aggregate to keep the app stable and improve it, and is not used to personally identify you.

How We Use Information

  • To provide the game's core features (levels, puzzles, themes, scores, and settings) on your device.
  • To understand general, anonymous usage trends and to diagnose crashes so we can improve stability and gameplay.

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services, which may collect information as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics) — collects anonymous usage statistics and crash/diagnostics data to keep the app stable. Privacy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play Services — https://policies.google.com/privacy

Internet Access

The app connects to the internet only to send anonymous analytics and crash data to the services above, and when you tap a button that opens an external page (for example, to rate the app or view other apps by the developer). Those external pages are governed by their own privacy policies.

Data Retention & Security

App settings and game progress are stored locally on your device. We do not operate our own servers that store your personal files. Anonymous analytics and crash data are retained by Google per their policies. You remain in control and can delete local app data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will remove it.

Your Choices

  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization in your device's Google settings.
  • You can delete all local app data by clearing the app's storage or by uninstalling the app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
