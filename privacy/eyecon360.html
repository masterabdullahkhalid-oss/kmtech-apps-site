Privacy Policy - Eyecon 360

App: Eyecon 360

Package: com.km.eyecon360

Developer: Khalid Mustafa Tech

Effective date: March 26, 2026 (Last updated: November 19, 2026)

This Privacy Policy describes how Khalid Mustafa Tech ("we," "us," or "the Service Provider") handles information in connection with your use of the Eyecon 360 mobile application (the "Application"). The Application is provided free of charge and is intended for use "as is." By downloading, accessing, or using the Application, you acknowledge that you have read and agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect and Use

The Application is designed with user privacy as a priority and does not require account creation or sign-in of any kind.

The Application's core feature allows you to enter a phone number to initiate a direct chat through a third-party messaging service (WhatsApp). This phone number is used solely to open the chat session and is not stored, logged, or transmitted to our servers at any point.

The Application does not access, read, or request permission to your device contacts. The Application does not collect precise or approximate location data. The Application does not employ Artificial Intelligence (AI) or machine learning to process, analyze, or profile user data.

2. Third-Party Services

To keep the Application free, stable, and continuously improving, we rely on the following trusted third-party service providers, each of which may independently collect limited technical data in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — used to display advertisements within the Application. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, general device and usage information, and ad-interaction data to deliver relevant advertising.
  • Google Firebase Analytics — collects anonymized, aggregated usage data (such as screens viewed and features used) to help us understand and improve the user experience.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — collects crash and diagnostic information (such as device model, operating system version, and crash logs) to help us identify and resolve technical issues.
  • Google Play Services — a required dependency that supports advertising, analytics, and core application functionality.

This information is collected and processed by Google in accordance with Google's own Privacy Policy, available at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Advertising and Your Consent

The Application displays advertisements through Google AdMob. For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the Application uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain your consent prior to displaying personalized advertisements. Where applicable, you may choose between personalized and non-personalized ads.

Further information on AdMob's data practices is available at https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

4. Third-Party Platforms

The Application may redirect you to third-party messaging platforms, including WhatsApp, to facilitate direct communication. Your interaction with such platforms is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms of service, over which we have no control. Beyond the services listed in Section 2, the Application does not share your personal information with any third party.

5. Data Retention and Your Choices

As the Application does not store personal data on our servers, no data retention or deletion request is applicable to Application-specific information. You may manage advertising personalization preferences through your device's system settings, and you may discontinue use of the Application at any time by uninstalling it from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed to, nor intended for use by, children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that such information has been inadvertently collected, we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

7. Data Security

The Application does not retain your phone number, chat content, or any personal communication data. All chat-initiation actions are performed in real time, with no information stored on our systems.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. Any updates will be posted on this page along with a revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

9. Your Consent

By downloading, accessing, or using the Application, you consent to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact Khalid Mustafa Tech at masterabdullahkhalid@gmail.com.
