Flashlight - Shake & Alerts is a flashlight app with strobe, SOS, colors, shake toggle and call/message blink alerts. Flashlight - Shake & Alerts is developed by Khalid Mustafa Tech. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email, phone number or contacts.

  • Files & media you choose: the app processes/saves media on your device at your request. These files stay on your device unless you share them yourself.
  • Permissions (device access):
  • Camera - to control the device flash (flashlight). No photos or video are captured or stored.
  • Microphone - only for optional sound-reactive modes; audio is processed live on-device and never recorded or uploaded.
  • Phone state - only to detect incoming calls for 'Blink on Call'.
  • Notification access - only for 'Blink on WhatsApp', to detect WhatsApp notifications on-device; content is not stored or transmitted.
  • Internet / Network - to load ads.
  • Automatically collected (by ad/analytics SDKs): Advertising ID, approximate app-usage and diagnostics/crash data, as described under Third-Party Services.

How We Use Information

  • To provide the app's core features on your device.
  • To show ads that keep the app free (via Google AdMob).
  • To diagnose crashes and improve stability.

We do not sell your personal information.
