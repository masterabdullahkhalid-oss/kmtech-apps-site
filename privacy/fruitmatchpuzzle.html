Privacy Policy - Fruit Match Puzzle-Juicy Match

App: Fruit Match Puzzle-Juicy Match

Package: com.kmtech.fruitmatchpuzzle

Developer: Khalid Mustafa Tech

Effective date: 24 November 2026

Fruit Match Puzzle-Juicy Match is a casual match-3 puzzle game developed by Khalid Mustafa Tech. It is provided as a free, ad-supported game. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, or precise location.

  • No personal data you provide: The app does not ask you to enter any personal information.
  • Game data stored on your device: Your game progress, levels, scores, lives, and settings are saved locally on your device only. This data is not transmitted to us.
  • Permissions: The app uses only internet access (needed to load ads and send anonymous analytics). It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, or phone access.
  • Automatically collected (by Google SDKs): a mobile advertising identifier, an approximate location inferred from your IP address, device and diagnostics information, anonymous app-usage statistics, and ad-interaction and crash data, as described under Third-Party Services. This information is used to serve ads, understand usage, and keep the app stable, not to personally identify you.

How We Use Information

  • To provide the game's core features (levels, puzzles, scores, lives, and settings) on your device.
  • To display advertising that keeps the game free.
  • To understand general, anonymous usage trends and diagnose crashes so we can improve the game.

We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Depending on your region and consent choices, AdMob and its partners may collect and use an advertising identifier, IP-based approximate location, and ad-interaction data to deliver and measure ads. Where required, a consent message is shown through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before personalized ads are served; you can change available choices there or in your device's Google settings. Learn more:

  • Google AdMob privacy: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services, which may collect information as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
  • Google Firebase / Google Analytics for Firebase — collects anonymous usage statistics to help improve the game. Privacy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play Services — https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Retention & Security

Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not operate our own servers that store your personal files. Advertising, analytics, and diagnostics data are retained by Google per their policies. You remain in control and can delete local app data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Children's Privacy

This game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will remove it.

Your Choices

  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization in your device's Google settings.
  • You can review your advertising consent choices through the in-app privacy options where available.
  • You can delete all local app data by clearing the app's storage or by uninstalling the app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
