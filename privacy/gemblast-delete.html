Data Deletion - Gem Blast Match 3

App: Gem Blast Match 3

Package: com.kmtech.gemblast

Developer: Khalid Mustafa Tech

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Gem Blast Match 3 stores your game progress, scores, unlocked levels and settings on your own device. There is no Gem Blast Match 3 account and no developer server that holds your game data, so you are in full control and can delete it yourself at any time. This page explains how.

1. Reset progress inside the game

  • Open Gem Blast Match 3 and go to Settings (gear icon), if available.
  • Use any Reset option to clear your progress and scores.

2. Delete everything at once

  • Clear all app data: on your phone go to Settings > Apps > Gem Blast Match 3 > Storage > Clear data. This erases all saved progress, scores and settings held on the device.
  • Uninstall: removing Gem Blast Match 3 deletes all data the game stored on the device.

Advertising data

This app shows ads through Google AdMob. Ad networks collect their own technical ad data (such as your advertising ID and IP address) directly; the developer never receives or stores it. To limit or delete this data, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of personalised ads in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads, and manage retention under Google's own privacy controls.

Request deletion by email

Because no data is held on a developer server, there is nothing stored for us to delete. If you still want to send a deletion request, or need help removing data from your device, contact:

Email: masterabdullahkhalid@gmail.com

Please include the app name (Gem Blast Match 3) in your message. We will reply with the steps above and help you complete the deletion. We aim to respond within 30 days.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: all data and settings the app stored on your device, once you follow the steps above.
  • Not held by us: the developer keeps no server copy of your data. Ad-network technical data is held by Google under its own policy and retention periods.

For full details, see the Gem Blast Match 3 Privacy Policy.
