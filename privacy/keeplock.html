Privacy Policy - KeepLock – App Lock & Privacy

App: KeepLock – App Lock & Privacy

Package: com.kmtech.keeplock

Developer: Khalid Mustafa Tech

Effective date: 25 November 2026

KeepLock – App Lock & Privacy ("KeepLock," the "Application") is an on-device app-lock and privacy tool developed by Khalid Mustafa Tech. It lets you protect selected apps with a PIN, pattern, password or biometrics. KeepLock is a local-first application: it does not operate an account system or a server that receives your personal data. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email, phone number, or contacts. KeepLock does not include any advertising or analytics SDK and does not sell or share your data.

Everything KeepLock uses to work is stored locally on your device only:

  • Your lock secrets: your PIN, pattern or password are stored locally in the app's private, protected storage and are used only to unlock your protected apps. We never receive them.
  • Your protection settings: the list of apps you choose to lock, relock timing, theme, trusted Wi-Fi networks, and other preferences.
  • Intruder photos (optional): if you enable intruder capture, a photo may be taken with the front camera after failed unlock attempts. These photos are saved only on your device and are never uploaded to us.

Permissions and Why They Are Used

KeepLock requests only the permissions needed for the features you turn on. All processing happens on your device.

  • Biometric / Fingerprint (USE_BIOMETRIC): lets you unlock with the biometric hardware. Biometric data is handled entirely by Android's secure system; KeepLock never sees or stores your fingerprint or face data.
  • Camera: used only for the optional "intruder selfie" feature to photograph someone who enters a wrong code. Photos stay on your device. KeepLock does not record video or use the camera for any other purpose.
  • Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS) & Accessibility Service: used to detect which app is currently in the foreground so KeepLock can show the lock screen for apps you chose to protect. This detection happens on-device; KeepLock does not log your browsing or build a usage profile for sharing.
  • Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): to show the lock screen on top of a protected app.
  • Query installed apps (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES): to let you pick which installed apps to lock. The app list is used only inside KeepLock and is not transmitted.
  • Notification access (optional): to hide the content of notifications from locked apps until you unlock them.
  • Device Admin (optional): to enable anti-uninstall protection so the app cannot be easily removed while protection is active.
  • Location (ACCESS_FINE/COARSE/BACKGROUND_LOCATION, Wi-Fi state): used only for the optional "Trusted Wi-Fi" feature. On Android, reading the connected Wi-Fi network's name requires location permission. KeepLock uses this to automatically relax locking on networks you trust; it does not collect or transmit your location, and it does not track where you are.
  • Notifications, Boot completed, Exact alarm, Battery optimization, Foreground service: to keep protection running reliably after restarts and in the background.
  • Internet / Network state: used only to check for app updates. KeepLock does not send your settings, codes, app list, photos, or location over the internet.
  • Shizuku (optional): if you connect Shizuku, KeepLock can use it for stronger protection. Shizuku is a separate app governed by its own terms.

How We Use Information

  • To lock and unlock the apps you choose and apply your protection settings.
  • To provide optional features (intruder capture, trusted Wi-Fi, anti-uninstall, notification hiding) when you enable them.

All of this happens on your device. We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or sale.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data. KeepLock has no advertising or analytics partners. Information leaves your device only if you choose to export a backup or share a file through another app, in which case that destination handles it under its own policy.

Data Retention & Security

Your codes, settings, app list, trusted networks, and intruder photos remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. KeepLock uses Android's private app storage and secure APIs. The app disables cloud backup of its sensitive data (allowBackup=false) to help keep your lock secrets on-device. No method of storage is completely secure, so keep your device updated and protected.

Children's Privacy

KeepLock is a general-purpose security utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Choices

  • You can disable any optional feature (intruder capture, trusted Wi-Fi, device admin, notification access) at any time in the app's settings and revoke the related Android permission in system settings.
  • You can delete intruder photos and all local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling KeepLock.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
