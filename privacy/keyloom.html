Privacy Policy - Keyloom Keyboard

App: Keyloom Keyboard

Package: com.kmtech.keyloom

Developer: Khalid Mustafa Tech

Effective date: August 24, 2026 (Last updated: August 24, 2026)

This Privacy Policy applies to the Keyloom Keyboard mobile application (the "Application"), provided by Khalid Mustafa Tech (the "Service Provider"). The Application is provided "as is."

1. What Keyloom Keyboard does

Keyloom Keyboard is a customizable Android keyboard. It provides multilingual layouts, suggestions, gesture typing, voice-input access, themes, shortcuts, clipboard actions and optional device-local word learning.

2. Information processed by the Application

Keyloom Keyboard does not require an account, registration or login.

To provide keyboard functions, the Application processes text while you type. Typed text is delivered to the app or text field you are using. The Service Provider does not operate a server that receives or stores your keystrokes, messages, passwords or document contents.

Depending on the features you enable, the Application may process:

  • Typed characters and surrounding text needed for editing, autocorrection and suggestions
  • Words you choose to add to the personal dictionary
  • Abbreviations, shortcuts and keyboard preferences
  • Selected themes, layouts, languages and accessibility settings
  • Clipboard content only when you use a clipboard-related keyboard action

Personal dictionary entries, preferences and shortcuts are stored in the Application's private storage on your device.

3. Sensitive text fields

Android identifies password and other sensitive input fields to keyboard applications. Keyloom Keyboard is designed to respect the input-field information supplied by Android and the app in which you are typing. No keyboard can control how another app classifies its fields, so you should enter sensitive information only into apps and websites you trust.

4. Permissions

The current version uses only permissions needed for its active features:

  • Vibration: provides optional haptic feedback when keys are pressed.
  • Notifications: may show keyboard-related status, setup or locally generated crash notifications on supported Android versions. Android asks for notification permission where required.

The Application does not request unrestricted contacts, location, camera, microphone, storage or advertising-identifier permission. Android also creates an app-specific internal permission used to protect non-exported runtime components; other apps cannot use it to access your keyboard data.

5. Personal dictionary and contacts

Learned and manually added words are kept in Keyloom Keyboard's private, device-local dictionary. The current version does not request access to your phone contacts and does not build suggestions from contact names.

You can review or delete personal dictionary entries from the Application's settings. Clearing the Application's data or uninstalling it also removes locally stored Application data, subject to any Android backup that you later choose to restore.

6. Clipboard features

Clipboard actions are initiated by you and use Android's clipboard facilities. Clipboard content is processed on the device to perform the requested copy, cut, paste or selection action. Keyloom Keyboard does not upload clipboard content to a Service Provider server.

Other apps, Android and your device manufacturer may separately process clipboard data under their own policies.

7. Voice input

When you choose voice input, Keyloom Keyboard launches a voice-recognition service available on your device. The selected voice provider—not Keyloom Keyboard—may receive and process audio, recognized text, language information and related technical data under that provider's privacy policy.

Keyloom Keyboard itself does not request microphone permission and does not operate a speech-recognition server. Voice input occurs only after you activate it.

8. Backup, restore and sharing

The Application lets you manually export or restore selected settings and dictionary data through Android's system file picker. Export begins only after you choose the backup action and destination. A backup can contain keyboard preferences, shortcuts and personal dictionary entries selected for backup.

The Application also allows Android's standard app-backup mechanism. If device backup is enabled, Android or your chosen backup provider may store and restore eligible Application data under that provider's terms and privacy policy.

You are responsible for protecting exported files after they leave the Application's private storage.

9. Crash and diagnostic information

If the Application crashes, it may create a crash report locally on your device containing the stack trace, app and device information, memory information and recent Application log entries. The Application presents a user-controlled reporting flow. A report is not sent to the Service Provider unless you explicitly choose to share it through another app or service.

The receiving email, messaging, storage or other service handles the shared report under its own privacy policy. Review a report before sharing it because diagnostic context may contain information relevant to what the Application was doing before the crash.

10. Advertising, analytics and third-party SDKs

The current version of Keyloom Keyboard does not include an advertising or analytics SDK and does not display ads. It does not request internet permission for keyboard operation.

If advertising or another third-party service is added in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the applicable consent controls will be updated before that version is released. Where required, users will be offered legally required privacy choices.

11. Data sharing and sale

The Service Provider does not sell your personal data. The Service Provider does not receive your typed content, personal dictionary or clipboard content through an Application-operated server.

Information leaves the Application only when you initiate a feature that uses another provider, such as voice recognition, Android backup, file export, crash-report sharing or opening an external link. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Information may also be disclosed when legally required or when necessary to protect rights, safety and security.

12. Retention and deletion

Locally stored settings, shortcuts, learned words and diagnostic files remain on your device until they are removed through an available in-app control, clearing app data or uninstalling the Application. Exported files remain wherever you chose to save or share them. Android backup copies are retained according to your device and backup provider settings.

13. Children's privacy

Keyloom Keyboard is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children through an Application-operated server. If you believe a child has directly provided personal information to the Service Provider, contact the Service Provider so reasonable deletion steps can be taken.

14. Security

The Service Provider uses reasonable technical safeguards, including Android application-private storage for local dictionary data. No device, storage system or transmission method is completely secure. Keep your device protected, install updates and take care when exporting files or choosing third-party voice, backup and sharing services.

15. International processing

Keyloom Keyboard does not operate a server that processes your keyboard content. Third-party services you choose—such as voice recognition, backup or sharing providers—may process information in other countries under their own safeguards and policies.

16. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Application, legal requirements or third-party integrations change. The published policy will show a revised "Last updated" date. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law.
