Privacy Policy - Sink Hole - Teeter

App: Sink Hole - Teeter

Package: com.sinkhole.teeter

Developer: Khalid Mustafa Tech

Effective date: September 11, 2026 (Last updated: September 11, 2026)

This Privacy Policy describes how Sink Hole - Teeter ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our Android application.

Overview

Sink Hole - Teeter is an offline accelerometer-based maze game. The App itself does not require an account, does not collect names, email addresses, or any personal profile information, and does not store gameplay data outside your device.

The App does, however, display advertisements through Google AdMob, and third-party advertising partners may collect certain information as described below.

Information Collected by Third-Party Advertising Partners

We use Google AdMob to display ads, and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent for personalized advertising where required by law (for example, under GDPR in the EEA/UK, or similar regulations elsewhere).

When ads are shown, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect:

  • Advertising ID / device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location
  • Device information (model, OS version, language, network type)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This information is used to serve ads, measure ad performance, and, where you have given consent, personalize ads. We do not directly collect, view, or store this data ourselves — it is processed by Google in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and Google's Partner Policy (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

You can review or withdraw your ad-personalization consent at any time through the in-app consent form (shown on first launch), or by adjusting your device's ad settings at Google Ads Settings (https://adssettings.google.com/).

Permissions Used by the App

  • INTERNET: Required to load and display ads
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Lets the App check connectivity before requesting an ad
  • VIBRATE: Haptic feedback during gameplay (e.g., when the ball falls in a hole)
  • WAKE_LOCK: Keeps the screen responsive during active gameplay

The App also uses your device's accelerometer sensor to control the ball. Sensor readings are used only in real time to move the ball on screen and are never stored, transmitted, or shared.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If the App's audience settings are configured for a general/mixed audience, ads may be limited to non-personalized ads for users identified as children, in line with Google Play's Families Policy and applicable laws (e.g., COPPA).

Data Retention and Security

We do not operate any backend server or database for this App, so we do not retain any personal data on our own systems. Any data processed for advertising purposes is retained and secured according to Google's own data retention and security practices.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete data processed by our advertising partners. Since this data is processed by Google, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) to exercise these rights, or manage your ad preferences at Google Ads Settings (https://adssettings.google.com/).

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this same location with an updated "Last updated" date.
