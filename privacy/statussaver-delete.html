Data Deletion - Status Saver: Download & Share

App: Status Saver: Download & Share

Package: com.kmtech.statussaver

Developer: Khalid Mustafa Tech

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Status Saver: Download & Share saves saved statuses (photos and videos) and app settings on your own device. There is no Status Saver: Download & Share account and no developer server that stores your downloads, so you are in full control and can delete everything yourself at any time. This page explains how.

1. Delete downloaded files inside the app

  • Open Status Saver: Download & Share and go to your downloads / saved list.
  • Tap a file's menu and choose Delete to remove it.
  • You can also select several items and delete them together.

2. Delete files saved to your phone's storage

Status Saver: Download & Share saves saved statuses (photos and videos) into your device's shared storage (such as the Gallery or a Downloads folder). Deleting inside the app may not remove copies that are already in your Gallery. To remove those, open your Gallery or Files app, find the Status Saver: Download & Share folder, and delete the files there.

3. Delete everything at once

  • Clear all app data: on your phone go to Settings > Apps > Status Saver: Download & Share > Storage > Clear data. This erases the app's history and settings held on the device.
  • Uninstall: removing Status Saver: Download & Share deletes all data the app stored in its own storage.

Advertising data

This app shows ads through Google AdMob. Ad networks collect their own technical ad data (such as your advertising ID and IP address) directly; the developer never receives or stores it. To limit or delete this data, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of personalised ads in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads, and manage retention under Google's own privacy controls.

Request deletion by email

Because no data is held on a developer server, there is nothing stored for us to delete. If you still want to send a deletion request, or need help removing data from your device, contact:

Email: masterabdullahkhalid@gmail.com

Please include the app name (Status Saver: Download & Share) in your message. We will reply with the steps above and help you complete the deletion. We aim to respond within 30 days.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: all data and settings the app stored on your device, once you follow the steps above.
  • Kept until you remove it yourself: any copies already saved to your Gallery or Downloads folder (delete them in your Gallery or Files app)
  • Not held by us: the developer keeps no server copy of your data. Ad-network technical data is held by Google under its own policy and retention periods.

For full details, see the Status Saver: Download & Share Privacy Policy.
