Privacy Policy - Status Saver: Download & Share

App: Status Saver: Download & Share

Package: com.kmtech.statussaver

Developer: Khalid Mustafa Tech

Effective date: 30 October 2026 (Last updated: 30 October 2026)

This Privacy Policy explains how Status Saver: Download & Share ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the App. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. About the App

The App lets you view, save, and share statuses (photos and videos) that WhatsApp has already downloaded to your device when you viewed them. The App does not log in to WhatsApp, does not access your chats or contacts, and does not fetch statuses from the internet on your behalf.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not require you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not ask for your name, phone number, email, or contacts.
  • We do not upload your statuses, photos, videos, or files to any server. Anything you save stays on your device.

3. Device Storage & Media Access

To show and save statuses, the App needs permission to read media/files from your device storage (specifically the folder where WhatsApp keeps recently viewed statuses) and to save files to your device's gallery/downloads. This access is used only on your device to display and save media you choose. These files are never sent to us.

4. Information Collected Automatically (by third-party services)

The App uses trusted third-party services that may collect limited data to keep the App running, show ads, and fix crashes:

  • Google AdMob (ads): may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate device/usage information, and interaction data to serve and measure ads.
  • Google Firebase Analytics: collects anonymous app-usage events (e.g., screens opened, features used) to help us improve the App.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: collects crash and diagnostic information (device model, OS version, crash logs) so we can fix problems.
  • Google Play Services: required for ads, analytics, and core functionality.

This data is processed by Google under its own privacy policies.

5. Advertising & Your Consent

The App shows ads via Google AdMob. In the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and Switzerland, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can choose personalized or non-personalized ads, and you can reset your choice from your device's ad settings at any time.

6. Internet Permission

The App uses the internet only to load ads and send anonymous analytics/crash data. It does not transfer your personal files over the internet.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us so we can address it.

8. Data Security & Retention

We rely on the security provided by Google's services and your device's operating system. Since we do not store your personal data or files on our own servers, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on your behalf. Analytics and crash data held by Google are retained according to Google's policies.

9. Third-Party Links / Policies

Your use of the above services is also governed by their privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob / Ads: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact Us
