Data Deletion - TalkKeep (All Call Recorder)

App: TalkKeep (All Call Recorder)

Package: com.khalidmustafatech.talkkeep

Developer: Khalid Mustafa Tech

Last updated: November 25, 2026

TalkKeep stores your recordings, transcripts, notes and settings on your own device. There is no TalkKeep account and no TalkKeep server that holds your calls or transcripts, so you are in full control of your data and can delete it yourself at any time. This page explains exactly how.

1. Delete a single recording (and its transcript, notes and tags)

  • Open TalkKeep and go to the recording you want to remove.
  • Tap the recording's menu and choose Delete. It moves to Trash.
  • To remove it for good, open Trash and tap Empty trash. Items in Trash are also deleted automatically after 30 days.

2. Delete everything at once

  • In the app: open Trash and use Empty trash after deleting recordings, or delete recordings in bulk from the list.
  • Clear all app data: on your phone go to Settings > Apps > TalkKeep > Storage > Clear data. This erases every recording, transcript, note, tag and setting held by the app on the device.
  • Uninstall: removing TalkKeep deletes all data the app stored on the device.

3. Delete copies you saved to Google Drive

If you chose a Google Drive save location or turned on the optional encrypted backup, copies may be in your own Google Drive folder. TalkKeep cannot read or delete files in your Drive for you. To remove them, open the Google Drive app, go to the folder you selected for TalkKeep, and delete the files there.

4. Advertising data

TalkKeep shows ads through Google AdMob (with AppLovin mediation). These networks collect their own technical ad data (such as your advertising ID and IP address) directly; the developer never receives or stores it. To limit or delete this data, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of personalised ads in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads, and manage retention under Google's and AppLovin's own privacy controls.

5. Request deletion by email

Because no data is held on a developer server, there is nothing we store for us to delete. If you still want to send a deletion request, or need help removing data from your device, contact:

Email: masterabdullahkhalid@gmail.com

Please include the app name (TalkKeep) in your message. We will reply with the steps above and help you complete the deletion. We aim to respond within 30 days.

6. What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: all recordings, transcripts, speaker labels, notes, tags, stars and app settings stored on your device, once you follow the steps above.
  • Kept until you remove it yourself: any copy you saved to your own Google Drive (delete it in the Drive app).
  • Not held by us: the developer keeps no server copy of your recordings, transcripts, notes or contacts. Ad-network technical data is held by Google and AppLovin under their own policies and retention periods.

For full details, see the TalkKeep Privacy Policy.
