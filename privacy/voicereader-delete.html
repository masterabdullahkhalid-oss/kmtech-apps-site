Data Deletion - Voice Reader AI — Text to Audio

App: Voice Reader AI — Text to Audio

Package: com.Kmtech.voicereaderai

Developer: Khalid Mustafa Tech

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Voice Reader AI stores your library, reading positions, saved files and settings on your own device by default. There is no account required for core reading, and nothing is uploaded unless you turn on optional cloud sync. This page explains how to delete your data.

1. Delete items inside the app

  • Open Voice Reader AI, go to your library or files list, and delete items you no longer want.
  • Any files you downloaded or imported are saved to your device storage — open your Files app to delete those copies too.

2. Delete optional cloud-sync data

If you signed in for cross-device sync, your library list, reading positions and preferences are stored in a sync database. You can delete your synced data at any time from within the app (in the account or sync settings). Without signing in, nothing is synced anywhere.

3. Delete everything at once

  • Clear all app data: on your phone go to Settings > Apps > Voice Reader AI > Storage > Clear data. This erases the library, positions and settings held on the device.
  • Uninstall: removing Voice Reader AI deletes all data the app stored on the device (synced data must be deleted in-app before you uninstall).

Advertising data

This app shows ads through Google AdMob. Ad networks collect their own technical ad data (such as your advertising ID and IP address) directly; the developer never receives or stores it. To limit or delete this data, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of personalised ads in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads, and manage retention under Google's own privacy controls.

Request deletion by email

Because no data is held on a developer server, there is nothing stored for us to delete. If you still want to send a deletion request, or need help removing data from your device, contact:

Email: masterabdullahkhalid@gmail.com

Please include the app name (Voice Reader AI — Text to Audio) in your message. We will reply with the steps above and help you complete the deletion. We aim to respond within 30 days.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: your on-device library, reading positions, saved files and settings, once you follow the steps above.
  • Kept until you remove it yourself: any files saved to device storage (delete in Files app) and any optional cloud-sync data (delete it in-app)
  • Not held by us: the developer keeps no server copy of your data. Ad-network technical data is held by Google under its own policy and retention periods.

For full details, see the Voice Reader AI — Text to Audio Privacy Policy.
