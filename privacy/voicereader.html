Privacy Policy - Voice Reader AI

App: Voice Reader AI — Text to Audio

Package: com.Kmtech.voicereaderai

Developer: Khalid Mustafa Tech

Effective date: September 11, 2026 (Last updated: September 11, 2026)

This Privacy Policy applies to the Voice Reader AI mobile application (the "Application"), provided by Khalid Mustafa Tech (the "Service Provider"). The Application is provided "as is."

1. What Voice Reader AI does

Voice Reader AI is a text-to-speech reading application. It fetches text from sources you choose to enable (books, web fiction, news, research papers, RSS feeds, your own files, and similar) and reads it aloud using on-device and optional cloud neural voices. It also includes optional accessibility tools such as document scanning, form filling, and benefits-related reminders.

2. Information processed by the Application

Voice Reader AI does not require an account to use the core reading features. By default, the Application processes text and voice data on your device to render audiobooks. The Service Provider does not operate a server that stores the content of what you read unless you enable optional cloud sync.

Depending on the features you enable, the Application may process:

  • Text fetched from sources you add to your library
  • Your reading position, library list, and voice preferences
  • Words, tags, or notes you save (highlights, pronunciation entries)
  • Camera images and recognized text when you use document scanning
  • Microphone audio when you use voice-paced reading or voice notes
  • Sign-in tokens for sources that require them (GitHub, Google Drive, Notion, Royal Road, AO3)
  • Calendar events, only if you grant calendar access for the optional agenda feature

3. Optional cloud sync

If you choose to sign in for cross-device sync, the Application sends your email address and a magic sign-in code to a third-party sync database, and stores your library list, reading positions, and preferences there. This is entirely optional; without signing in, nothing is synced anywhere. You can delete your synced data at any time from within the Application.

4. Content sources

The Application connects directly to the servers of any content source you enable (for example, an online fiction site, a code-hosting platform, or an RSS feed) exactly as a web browser would. The Service Provider does not act as an intermediary and does not see the content of these requests. Where a source requires sign-in, credentials are stored encrypted on your device and sent only to that source's own servers.

5. Camera and document scanning

The Application can use the device camera to scan printed pages, forms, or documents. Camera access is requested only when you start a scan, with an on-screen explanation beforehand. Text recognition runs entirely on-device; scanned images and recognized text are not uploaded or shared unless you explicitly export or share the result yourself. Declining camera access does not block the feature — you can select an existing photo instead.

6. Microphone

The Application uses the microphone only for features you actively start: recording a voice note, narrating your own audio, or a voice-paced reading mode. Microphone access is requested at the time you use one of these features, not at launch. Recorded audio and any speech-to-text transcription are processed on-device and are not uploaded unless you explicitly choose to share a transcript (for example, tapping "Summarize" with your own connected AI provider).

7. Sensitive documents and personal information

Optional tools let you save copies of personal documents (ID, proof of income, benefit letters) and household details (name, address, income) so the Application can help pre-fill forms. This data is stored encrypted on your device, is protected behind your device's screen lock, and is never uploaded, synced, or shared. You can view, edit, or permanently delete it at any time. Social Security Numbers are never saved by the Application.

8. Permissions

The current version requests the following permissions for its active features:

  • Internet / network state: to fetch content, download voices, and sync (if enabled)
  • Camera: for document/page scanning (optional, requested at time of use)
  • Microphone: for voice notes and voice-paced reading (optional, requested at time of use)
  • Notifications: for playback controls and optional reminders
  • Exact alarms: to deliver deadline reminders you set, at the time you chose
  • Notification policy access: only if you enable automatic Do Not Disturb during the sleep timer
  • Foreground service: to keep audio playing and background tasks running reliably
  • Boot completed: to re-arm your reminders after a device restart
  • Calendar access: optional, only to narrate your device calendar as an agenda

The Application does not request location, contacts, or unrestricted storage access.

9. Advertising

The Application shows ads (via Google AdMob), which may include banner, interstitial, rewarded, and app-open ad formats. Google and its advertising partners may collect an advertising identifier and other technical data to serve and measure ads, under Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can manage ad personalization from your device's Google Settings. The Service Provider does not receive or store the data used for ad targeting.

10. Optional AI features and bring-your-own-key services

Some features (AI chat about what you're reading, cloud text-to-speech voices) work only if you supply your own API key for a third-party provider (such as an AI or cloud voice service). When used, your request and key are sent directly to that provider under their own privacy policy. The Service Provider does not proxy, log, or have access to this traffic.

11. Data sharing and sale

The Service Provider does not sell your personal data. Information leaves the Application only when you use a feature that connects to another provider (a content source, cloud sync, a bring-your-own-key AI/voice service, or when you explicitly export or share something). Those providers process data under their own terms. Information may also be disclosed where legally required.

12. Retention and deletion

Locally stored data (library, settings, scanned documents, saved profile, reminders) remains on your device until removed through an in-app control, by clearing app data, or by uninstalling the Application. If you use optional cloud sync, your synced record remains until you delete it in-app or sign out and request deletion.

13. Children's privacy

Voice Reader AI is a general-purpose reading tool and is not directed to children under 13. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Content sources you enable may include user-generated material not filtered by the Application; a parent managing the app for a child should review and disable sources accordingly.

14. Security

Sensitive data (documents, saved credentials, tokens) is stored using Android's encrypted storage facilities, with document access additionally gated behind your device's screen lock. All network requests use HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure; keep your device and app updated.

15. International processing

The Application itself does not operate a server that processes your reading content, beyond the optional sync database described above. Third-party services you choose to use (content sources, AI providers, ad networks) may process data in other countries under their own policies.

16. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the Application, legal requirements, or third-party integrations change. The published policy will show a revised "Last updated" date. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law.
